How the score is calculated

The score is derived entirely from public, on-chain activity indexed from the Stellar network — no off-chain data, no self-reported information. It measures how a wallet has actually used DeFi protocols over time.

Two scoring paths

Every wallet is scored one of two ways, depending on what it's done on-chain. Whichever applies, only the highest-signal path is used — a wallet is never scored both ways.

Lending path

Used when a wallet has at least one completed borrow → repay cycle on a lending protocol (currently Blend). This is the strongest signal available, since repaying debt is the clearest evidence of creditworthiness — so this path can reach tier A.

Activity path

Used when a wallet has no lending cycles, but has meaningful breadth or volume of other DeFi activity (swaps, liquidity provision, yield deposits, transfers). This path can reach tier A only above a high score threshold — most activity-only wallets land in tier B.

Floor

Used when a wallet has very little indexed activity (fewer than 3 meaningful events and fewer than 2 protocols). Score is simply capped at a low tier-C value based on event count.

Lending path breakdown

Example line: LENDING path: 3 cycles, speed 34, cyclecount 12, income 8 [participation 2, months 3 (2pts), net $61.40 realized (4pts)], tenure 11, raw 67

TermWhat it means
cyclesNumber of completed borrow→repay cycles found (a debt opened and later fully closed).
speedPoints for how quickly debt was typically repaid, from the median cycle duration — faster repayment scores higher (bucketed: within an hour, a day, 3/7/14/30/90 days, or longer).
cyclecountPoints for having multiple cycles: min(cycles, 5) × 4 — more repeat, completed borrowing is rewarded, up to 5 cycles.
incomePoints for real yield income (see "How income is scored" below) — up to 10 points here: 2 for participating at all, 4 for how many distinct months had realized income, 4 for how much relative to what was put in.
tenurePoints for how long ago the wallet's cycle activity spans (7/30/90/180+ days → 0/3/7/11/15 pts) — longer track record scores higher.
distribution / rewardsPoints from recurring distribution claims and from incentive-reward claims — see the sections below.
rawSum of the above. If a wallet has only 1 cycle, the score is provisionally capped lower until a second cycle confirms the pattern.

Activity path breakdown

Example line: ACTIVITY path: protocols 5, events 784, days 44, breadth 15, volume 25, consistency 20, tenure 15, income 6 [participation 2, months 4 (3pts), net $128.07 realized (1pts)], raw 81

TermWhat it means
protocolsNumber of distinct DeFi protocols the wallet has touched (e.g. Blend, Soroswap, Aquarius, Sushi).
eventsCount of "meaningful" on-chain events — everything except generic contract calls with no clear economic action.
daysNumber of distinct calendar days the wallet was active on.
breadthPoints for protocol diversity: min(protocols × 5, 15).
volumePoints for activity volume: min(events, 25) — capped so very high-frequency wallets don't automatically dominate the score.
consistencyPoints for how spread out the activity is across distinct days (bucketed: 2/6/13/29/89+ days → 0/4/8/14/20/26 pts).
tenurePoints for the time span between the wallet's first and last meaningful event (7/30/90/180+ days → 0/3/7/11/15 pts).
incomePoints for real yield income (see "How income is scored" below) — up to 12 points here: 2 for participating at all, 5 for how many distinct months had realized income, 5 for how much relative to what was put in.
rawSum of the above.

From raw score to final tier

The raw point total isn't the displayed score — it's run through a scaling curve that concentrates most wallets in the middle of the range and reserves the top end for genuinely strong track records, then mapped to a tier:

Tier A
67 – 100
Tier B
34 – 66
Tier C
0 – 33

The lending path and activity path use separate scaling curves calibrated to their own raw-score ranges, which is why the same raw number can map to different final scores depending on path.

How income is scored

Beyond lending and activity, the score also looks at real yield/savings income — deposits and withdrawals on yield products like DeFindex vaults, Stellar DeFi Hub, and Upshift/Gami. This only counts realized income: USD a wallet actually withdrew above what it had deposited into that position, not paper gains from a balance that's still parked. A wallet that deposits and never withdraws gets a small flat credit for participating, but the bulk of the income score comes from cashing out more than was put in.

Amounts across different assets (USDC, EURC, XLM, and tokenized bonds like CETES, TESOURO, USTRY) are converted to USD using live prices, refreshed every 6 hours from Kraken (for XLM/EURC) and Etherfuse's own pricing API (for the tokenized sovereign bonds, which each track their own local-currency bond value plus that currency's FX rate — not a simple $1 peg).

TermWhat it means
participationA small flat bonus (2 points) just for having any yield deposit/withdraw history at all, whether or not income has been realized yet.
months (Npts)Number of distinct calendar months in which the wallet realized net-positive income — rewards income spread over time, not one lucky cash-out.
net $X realized (Npts)Total USD withdrawn above total USD deposited, across all yield positions. Scored as a rate relative to how much was deposited, so a small active saver and a large passive one are compared fairly.

Distribution income

Some wallets receive income a different way: a recurring claim from a mass-distribution / payroll-style contract (e.g. Fundable), rather than depositing into and withdrawing from a yield position. A single claim has no deposit side to measure a "return" against, so it can't be scored like yield income. It is weighted toward consistency over magnitude instead: a wallet claiming something every month looks like real, recurring income, while a single claim ever looks like a one-time windfall and scores accordingly low. Claims are excluded from the breadth/volume/consistency numbers above, but claims spanning 2 or more distinct months can qualify a wallet for the activity path on their own.

TermWhat it means
participationA small flat bonus for having any claim history. Up to 4 points on the lending path and 6 on the activity path across all distribution terms.
months (Npts)Distinct calendar months with a claim, saturating at 6 — the main driver of this score.
$X claimed (Npts)Total USD value claimed, saturating at $500 — a smaller, secondary factor.

Incentive rewards

Protocols also pay out reward tokens: AQUA to Aquarius liquidity providers (plus Aquarius gauge rewards, paid in assets like USDC and XLM) and BLND to Blend lenders and backstop depositors, and reward tokens to Phoenix liquidity providers who stake their LP tokens (Phoenix doesn't record the amount of a reward claim, so those count toward the months-claimed factor only, never the dollar one). These are scored as their own signal, and deliberately the smallest of the three income signals (at most 3 points on the lending path and 4 on the activity path). Rewards are incentives, not organic income: they scale with how much a wallet has staked, they are volatile, and some wallets simply rotate between incentive programs. So, as with distribution income, consistency (distinct months with a claim, saturating at 8) counts for more than size (total USD claimed, saturating at $50). Reward tokens are converted to USD with live prices (DefiLlama), refreshed every 6 hours. A claim that can't be priced still counts as participation but adds no dollars. Liquidity-provider fee claims are a different thing and are not counted here.

TermWhat it means
participation1 point for having claimed any reward.
months (Npts)Distinct calendar months with a reward claim — up to 1 point (lending) or 2 (activity).
$X in rewards (Npts)Total USD value of rewards claimed — up to 1 point.

A few notes

  • Scores are computed live from indexed on-chain history on every lookup — nothing is cached or pre-stored per wallet.
  • Very high-activity wallets (e.g. automated trading bots, market makers) are capped at 50,000 most-recent events per lookup for performance — this rarely changes the outcome, since the volume, breadth and consistency components already cap out well before that.
  • This tool only sees activity the indexer has captured (currently Blend, Soroswap, Aquarius, Sushi, DeFindex and a growing set of others) — it is not a complete picture of a wallet's full on-chain history.
  • This is not a regulated credit score and isn't financial advice — it's an experimental measure of on-chain DeFi behavior.
