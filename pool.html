USDC credit pool — testnet

A USDC lending pool with credit lines priced on-chain from your LCRD score, on Stellar testnet. Lenders deposit USDC for shares; borrowers draw an unsecured line (from their score) or a secured line (against their own deposit). Interest accrues by the second and repayments build an on-chain record. The Soroban port of the Arc credit product.
